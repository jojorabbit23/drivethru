SERVING ROOM SOURCE TEXTURES
16 separate PNG base-colour textures and graphics cropped from generated concept atlas. Native resolution about 313px per graphic. These are stylized source art, not mesh-fitted UV textures or a PBR set. No normal, roughness or metallic maps included. Tile seams have not been corrected; repeat surfaces may show seams. Cabinet bevels, conduit and packaging folds are painted into the images. Use posters and magazine covers on flat planes; adjust UVs to each crop aspect ratio. In Godot use nearest filtering for a pixel finish. Use actual geometry for depth and handles.
