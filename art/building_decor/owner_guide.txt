The Drive Thru: Building Decoration Guide
Every decoration below is its own placeable object with a unique ID (AREA_name). Type says how to build it: Prop (mesh), Decal (texture on a surface), Sign (decal or flat mesh with text), Light, Audio (emitter), Fungal (growth mesh or decal). Behaviour lists interaction and damage states; a dash means purely static.

Tying decoration to gameplay
• Decorations get damaged as the barricades fall. Windows crack, posters tear, and the roof sign loses letters, so the building shows how the night is going.
• Hide a small recurring "Employee of the Month" gag that updates each shift.
• Make some props interactive, like the ticket rail, the bell, and the freezer door, so the world feels worked in rather than just looked at.
