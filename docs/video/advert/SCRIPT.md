Intro Cutscene Script: Zombie Drive-Thru
FADE IN

INT. DRIVE-THRU KITCHEN OFFICE - DAY

A battered old radio crackles somewhere in the room, playing upbeat 90s-style music that feels strangely cheerful compared to the chaos outside.

FIRST PERSON POV

Boots stretched out on a desk.

A cigarette smoulders between gloved fingers.

In your lap sits a glossy magazine:

"MUSHROOM MONTHLY"

The cover proudly displays a giant oyster mushroom.

Pages flip lazily.

Outside the window, distant screams echo across the wasteland.

You don't look up.

Just another day.

The radio continues playing.

Then...

RIIIIING.

An old wired phone erupts beside you.

You sigh.

Reach behind the chair without looking.

Grab the receiver.

Bring it to your ear.

A burst of panicked, squeaky static and unintelligible rambling comes through.

SQUIRLY CUSTOMER (PHONE) (distorted frantic noises)

"SKRRT! CHK-CHK! EEEE! HRRRRK!"

You nod as if it all makes perfect sense.

PLAYER "Yurrrp. No problem."

Pause.

PLAYER "Five minutes."

Beat.

A grin forms.

PLAYER "MAX."

You slam the phone down.

Instantly launch from the chair.

The music kicks louder.

A well-worn pump-action shotgun is snatched from a rack.

CHK-CHAK.

You spin.

BOOM.

A mushroom zombie bursts through the office window.

Its fungus-covered head explodes into spores and chunks.

The body collapses out of sight.

Without breaking stride, you head into the kitchen.

KITCHEN
You pass the prep station.

A raw burger patty is grabbed.

Tossed casually over one shoulder.

The camera follows it through the air.

It lands perfectly on the sizzling grill.

SSSSSSSS.

Outside the serving window, more mushroom zombies stagger into view.

One.

Three.

Five.

A whole crowd gathering.

You ignore them.

The burger comes first.

The patty sizzles.

You lean in.

Turn it.

Perfect.

Movement appears in the corner of your eye.

A zombie lunges through the side window.

Without looking fully away from the grill, you grab a kitchen knife.

THUNK.

Straight into its face.

The zombie collapses.

You pull the knife free.

Flip the burger.

Back to work.

ORDER PREP
Bun.

Burger.

Cheese.

Sauce.

Wrap.

Box.

Fast.

Practised.

Military precision.

Outside, more zombies begin swarming the building.

Hands slap against windows.

Glass starts cracking.

You close the burger box.

Done.

CUSTOMER WINDOW
A short walk through the kitchen.

Bodies of dead zombies scattered around.

The radio still playing like nothing's wrong.

You reach the service window.

A gigantic apocalypse-modified truck waits outside.

Engine growling.

Armoured plating.

Dents from zombie impacts.

The customer's hand emerges holding cash.

You hand over the burger.

Transaction complete.

Customer drives away.

For one glorious second...

Silence.

A low growl emerges behind you.

You slowly turn around.

The kitchen is packed.

Dozens of mushroom zombies.

Every entrance breached.

Every window shattered.

The entire restaurant flooded with infected.

A sea of fungal flesh.

The shotgun hangs loosely at your side.

The radio music continues.

You tighten your grip.

PLAYER "Ah."

Pump the shotgun.

CHK-CHAK.

PLAYER "Rush hour."

The horde charges.

CUT TO BLACK.
