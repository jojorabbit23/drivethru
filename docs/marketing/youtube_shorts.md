## Posting tips
- Post them a day or two apart rather than all at once.
- The first frame is the cover in the Shorts feed, so each one opens on action and its hook headline is visible from frame one.
- The captions and headline sit away from the bottom and right edges, where YouTube's buttons and channel name go.
