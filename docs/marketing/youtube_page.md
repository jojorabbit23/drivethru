## Description
The zombies want in. The survivors want burgers. You're the only one working tonight.

THE DRIVE THRU is a first-person zombie siege meets burger joint. Hold your roadside drive-through against the horde, harvest the glowing mushrooms they drop, grill them into patties and serve them to the armoured survivors who pull up to your window.

🍔 KILL – Board up the windows and fight off Shamblers, fast Scuttlers and the lance-armed Lancer with your knife, pistol and pump shotgun.
🍄 COOK – Every zombie drops a glowing mushroom: common, blue or rare red. Drop them on the grill, press them, flip them and pull them off before they burn.
🚚 SERVE – Survivors roll up in armoured buses and trucks. Build their order (singles, doubles and the Red + Blue Special) and get it to the window while it's fresh for the best tips.
🔧 UPGRADE – Spend your takings on ammo, a bigger grill, a heated holding tray and a drive-thru bell.

You play Cleatimus Maximus, redneck mushroom farmer turned apocalypse fry cook. Open all night. Mostly.

Currently in development, built in Godot. All footage is captured in-engine.
