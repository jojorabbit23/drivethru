The Endgame Flow

Late-game gameplay should look like:

Plain Text
Hear Customer
 
Check Order
 
Throw Patty On Grill
 
Shoot Window 1
 
Repair Boards
 
Defend Runner
 
Collect Mushrooms
 
Grab Cooked Patty
 
Bag Burger
 
Serve Customer
 
Buy Ammo
 
Repeat

The player is never standing still cooking.

The grill is always cooking in the background while they're defending the restaurant.

That's the key thing that will make the system fun instead of turning into a restaurant simulator. The cooker is effectively another "battlefront" the player has to monitor, just like a barricade or a delivery runner.
