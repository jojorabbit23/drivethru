# Mushroom-headed scientist infected

The owner's static mushroom-headed scientist GLB now has a complete 18-bone infected rig, normalized skin weights, automatic mesh LODs and seven clips: idle, shamble, rip, climb, swing, flinch and death. The lab coat, teal trousers, orange tie and mushroom textures are retained. Source is enemies/scientist/source/scientist.glb. Rebuild with tools/build_scientist.gd; never edit generated scientist_model.tscn, scientist_body.res, scientist_live.res, scientist_head.res or scientist_material.res directly.

Skinning uses source landmarks and welded topology smoothing to avoid treating the long coat and hanging hands as one limb. Ground contact is baked into ordinary clips; climbing retains its authored motion. This work happens in the builder, with no runtime per-vertex correction. While alive, a continuous skinned mesh keeps the neck intact. Only death swaps in the headless mesh and exposes orange wet cut caps. The detachable head retains its own rigid mesh and headshot area. The walk uses a one-second alternating IK stride with flat boots through 62% of each step; sole weights are rigid and ankle blending remains smooth.

Gameplay: 100 HP, 12 damage per claw swing, 3x headshot vulnerability, normal stagger, lane movement, barricade ripping, window climbing, navigation pursuit and death through the shared Shambler API. A pistol-strength headshot kills it. scientist_every defaults to 4, after existing Lancer/Scuttler priority; it replaces an ordinary scheduled slot, preserves total wave counts and respects the crowd cap. B spawns one for testing. Cooking mushroom drops and the shared 7.5% E-use health-bottle roll are retained.

Every death hides the attached head and launches one complete fungal head from the current animated bone pose. It tumbles and settles against world geometry with one damped box physics body, no player/enemy collision layer, and no zombie group or rewards. At most six detached heads exist at once; they clean up after roughly five seconds. The headless body uses the regular corpse animation and eight-second lifetime. Repeated damage cannot duplicate heads, kills or drops. Detachment also emits one fungal splatter burst at the current animated neck cut, using the existing globally capped Ooze droplets and decals. No extra lights or persistent emitter are added.

Quiet spatial fungal breaths/attack grunts, a stem snap and first-contact landing sound are generated by tools/audio/make_scientist_audio.py. Effects bus, distance attenuation, no added lights or particles. Generated audio sources and PCM peaks are deterministic.

Findings: simply cutting the source mesh and assigning hard limb regions caused coat/hand stretch. Welded seam-consistent weight smoothing reduced sampled maximum triangle edge growth to 0.124 m; sampled floor-contact error is 0.005 m. The detached head uses its exact animated transform and a centred collider, preserving placement when the scientist dies during another animation.

Verification: tools/scientist_test.gd covers skin normalization/deformation, clips and contact, flat stance-foot planting, intact living neck/death-only cut, one-time bounded neck spray, hitbox/headshots, stagger/attacks, head detachment/gravity/cap/cleanup, one-time cash/drop bookkeeping, both window climbs and scheduled wave accounting. tools/capture_scientist.gd renders native Vulkan poses plus falling/landed head and asserts cleanup. Full regression suites, import/script checks and required room/weapon captures also run before publication.
