# Serving room visual rework

Built from the two owner-supplied images retained in art/serving_room/. tools/serving_room.gd defines the room treatment and atlas-cell material specs; tools/shop_decor.gd calls it from shop_floor(). The later Serving_Room_Texture_Pack.zip is extracted unchanged into art/serving_room/pack/; its 16 individual PNGs now drive the materials directly with nearest/mipmap filtering. tools/build_shop.gd supports these explicit source paths, writes their materials, then rebuilds the shop scene and GLB. The original atlas-region mapping remains as a builder fallback; its superseded generated cell textures are not included. Original atlas/reference are not painted over. Rebuild with the usual --import then tools/build_shop.gd. Generated materials/serve_*.tres and levels/shop/generated are outputs, not hand-edited sources.

Changes: room-only tiled wall/floor/ceiling finish skins, teal cabinet fronts and orange pulls, weathered steel counter, scaled perforated mat, industrial fridge, stocked shelving, pin-up/brand posters, No Refunds and Eat Local signs, burger bags/cartons, magazines, small warmer and extinguisher. Posters and packaging are the supplied art. Only the existing room lights and actual warmer lamp emit light.

Service counter/hatch height and location, shelf/fridge/bin collision footprints and player-clip volumes are preserved. New finish skins and small decorative props add no collision. Room doors and working approaches stay open. Cooking/customer station scripts are unchanged. The rebuilt whole scene also retains all other rooms and existing navigation generation.

Verified edited scripts with check-only, rebuilt the scene/materials through their builder, and ran all 13 suites (including movement, cooking/service economy, pickups, enemies, weapons and menu). Rendered and inspected serving-room overview/counter plus customer hatch and required knife capture. docs/previews/serving_room.png is the current room overview. The reference is adapted to the existing shop footprint and game art rather than a pixel-identical reproduction.
