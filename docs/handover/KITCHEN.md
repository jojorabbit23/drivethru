# Kitchen visual rework

The 16 owner-supplied PNGs from Kitchen_Texture_Pack.zip are retained unchanged with their README under art/kitchen/pack/. Both room references are retained beside them. tools/kitchen_room.gd provides the material specs and geometry; tools/shop_decor.gd delegates kitchen() to it; tools/build_shop.gd writes materials and rebuilds the whole generated shop scene/GLB. Rebuild with --import, then tools/build_shop.gd. Do not hand-edit generated materials or the scene.

The kitchen has room-only splattered wall tiles, dirty checker floor and stained ceiling; weathered steel workstations and fridge shell; teal fridge doors, handles and ventilation; a cooker hood with visible front/underside slats; hanging utensils; labelled CAPS/STALKS/SPORES jars, ingredient bottles and shelf stock; chopping board and loose mushrooms; orange towels, packaging, recipe boards and a real polygon clock; a south-east sink with rim, tap, drainboard trays, legs and a working mat. The live grill and patties are still controlled by game/grill.gd: no painted food replaces its timing or interaction.

Existing north workstation, griddle, fridge and rack footprints and griddle height are preserved. A new sink collider/clip sits against the south-east wall, away from the middle doorway and repair window. The wall skins cut around both doors and the actual east breach window; no new decoration blocks the window's open path. The room light was reduced from 1.5 to 1.05 and hood task light from 0.8 to 0.65 after rendering to reduce washed-out white tiles; only actual lights emit. Palette-based tints and the supplied textures retain the teal/orange style.

One ooze regression sample found a narrow frame gap between regular surface probes. core/ooze.gd now probes its exact visible 60% corners and centre as well as the existing grid, so floating splat corners are rejected/shrunk. The gameplay test itself was not weakened. All 13 test suites were rerun, including movement, ooze, cooking/customer loop, enemies, pickups, firearms, knife/dagger and menu. Edited scripts pass check-only. Native Compatibility renders of both kitchen views, the functional grill/hatch and required knife capture were inspected.

Previews: docs/previews/kitchen_cooking.png and kitchen_sink.png. This adapts the references to the existing game footprint and low-poly geometry; the ingredient jars and sink are decorative and do not add new cooking mechanics.
